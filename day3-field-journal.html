      🧩 Text sections and features

      📋 Florida B.E.S.T. (Grade 6):
      ELA.6.R.2.1 Explain how individual text sections and/or features convey meaning in texts.

      Every part has a job

      To see how a part adds meaning, ask: “What does this add to the big idea?”

      • 🏷️ Subheadings name the topic of a section.
      • 📊 Diagrams and labels show what the words describe.
      • ⏳ Timelines show events in order or how things change.
      • 📦 Sidebars and glossaries explain terms or add related facts.
      • 🔤 Bold words tell you a key term is being defined.

      Sections also work together. One section can set up the problem, and the next can show the result.

      Your teacher can write here during the lesson. You can write too if you want!

      🌐 Research a question

      📋 Florida B.E.S.T. (Grade 6):
      ELA.6.C.4.1 Conduct research to answer a question, drawing on multiple reliable and valid sources, and refocusing the inquiry when appropriate.

      Be a research detective

      1. Ask a focused questionNot too big, not yes or no. “How do service dogs help people?” works better than “Dogs?”
      2. Use more than one sourceCompare at least two. Do they agree?
      3. Check if it’s reliableWho wrote it? Are they an expert? Is it up to date? Does it list sources? Is it trying to teach you or sell you something?
      4. RefocusToo many results? Make your question narrower. Too few? Try new key words or a bigger idea.
